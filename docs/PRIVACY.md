# Privacy and children's data (NDPA 2023)

WardPay processes personal data about children and their guardians on behalf
of schools. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 the **school is the data
controller** and WardPay is its **data processor**. This page records how the
system meets that responsibility. It is updated as features are added.

## What we store

| About | Data | Why | Protection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student | Admission number, names, class per term, status | Billing and receipts | School-scoped; staff access by role |
| Student (optional) | Gender, date of birth | Schools' own records | Optional; leave blank if not needed |
| Guardian | Name, relationship to the child | Addressing notices | School-scoped |
| Guardian | Phone, email | Receipts, reminders, parent sign-in; linking a parent's own records across branches of one school group | **Encrypted at rest**; looked up by a keyed blind index |
| Guardian | When and how consent was given | NDPA accountability | Recorded on every guardian |

No BVNs, NINs, photos, addresses, religion or health data are collected.

## Principles applied in the design
- **Minimum data.** Only what fee collection needs. Gender and date of birth
  are optional.
- **Encryption.** Guardian phone and email (and staff MFA secrets) are
  encrypted at rest (`app/core/crypto.py`). Lookups use a keyed HMAC of the
  normalised phone, so the database never holds phone numbers in plain text.
- **Consent.** Staff must confirm the school holds the guardian's consent
  before guardian details are saved, whether one at a time or by spreadsheet
  import. The time and method are recorded on the guardian.
- **Audit.** Changes to a guardian's contact details are audited without
  storing the numbers themselves, because the phone controls parent-portal
  access.
- **Logs.** Logs never contain OTPs, tokens, BVNs or full account numbers
  (a redaction filter enforces this).
- **Isolation.** Each school's data is visible only to that school's staff and
  to the guardians linked to each child. A parent with children at two schools
  is a separate guardian record in each; the schools never see each other's records.

## Receipts
Printed and PDF receipts carry the student's name, admission number and class,
because they're given to the parent. The public verification page behind the
QR code shows only the school, receipt number, amount, date, status and the
student's initials.

## Parent portal
Parents sign in with the phone number the school holds for them. We store a
hash of each sign-in code for at most a day, and their portal sessions for 30
days after they end. A parent sees only their own children. Sign-in codes are
sent by SMS through Termii (a processor).

## Family payment links
Bills and reminders carry a link that opens a payment page without signing in.
Anyone the parent forwards it to sees the school, each child's **first name**,
class, what's owed and the account number, nothing else (no surnames, no
admission numbers, no parent details). It expires after about a term, the school
can replace it at once, and erasing the parent ends it.

## Pay cards
Schools can print a card per child to send home: the school, the child's full name,
class and admission number, the child's account number, and this term's fees with
what's left to pay. It carries no parent details and no payment link.

## Messages to parents
Receipts, bill notices and reminders go to the child's main parent only. Each
message's text is kept in the school's message log so staff can see what was
sent. Parents can turn off bill notices and reminders in the portal (or ask the
school to). Receipts still go, because they record the parent's own payment.
No messages are sent at night. Messages travel through Meta (WhatsApp), Termii
(SMS) or the school's email provider, each a processor. They carry the
parent's and child's names, amounts and the child's payment account number,
and nothing else.

Parents who prefer it can also get **phone calls** (the school marks them, or
they choose it in the portal, and can stop it the same way). A call says the
school's name, the child's first name, the amount and the account number; the
words are generated by the voice provider (Africa's Talking, a processor) while
the call is live. We store the parent's call preference and language, and the
call's text in the message log (cleared with other message texts).

## Reports
The debtors list shows each main parent's name and phone number so the school
can call about fees. Only the proprietor and bursar can see it. Every Excel or
PDF download of it is recorded in the school's activity log with who took it,
so a school can account for copies of parents' details that leave the system.

## School sign-up and the platform
A school that signs up but never confirms its email is deleted, with its proprietor's details, after `PENDING_SCHOOL_DAYS` (14). The platform's audit trail keeps only the school's name and a masked email. Platform admins see each school's figures (pupil counts, money), not pupils' or parents' details. Support lookups show a student's initials and admission number at most, and every lookup is recorded.

## Shared with payment gateways
To issue a student's account number, the gateway (Paystack) receives the
student's first and last name, the primary guardian's phone number, and the
guardian's email (or a routing address we generate if there isn't one). Card
payments send the payer's email to the gateway. Gateways are processors under
their own NDPA obligations. No BVNs are sent or stored, and card details never
reach WardPay.

## Data subjects' rights
- **Access:** parents download everything held about them and their children from the portal ("Download my data"); staff can export it for a parent who asks in person. Every export is recorded.
- **Prepared downloads** (the journal, the end-of-session pack): built in the background, kept for one day, then deleted; only the person who asked can download them, and each download is recorded.
- **Accountant's journal:** identifies pupils by admission number only (no names or parent details), so the school can hand it to an outside accountant.
- **Erasure:** a school admin or proprietor can erase a parent (once none of their children is a current pupil) or a former pupil. Personal details and message texts are removed; bills and payments stay, anonymised, because schools must keep financial records for six years. **Issued receipts keep the name they were issued with**: they are append-only financial records, the same as a paper receipt book.
- **Objection to messages:** parents turn off bill notices and reminders in the portal.
- **Consent:** recorded on every guardian (when and how), whether added one at a time or by import.

## Retention
| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Parent sign-in codes | A day |
| Ended staff and parent sessions | 30 days |
| Unconfirmed school sign-ups | 14 days (`PENDING_SCHOOL_DAYS`) |
| Text of messages to parents | 365 days (`MESSAGE_BODY_RETENTION_DAYS`); who, when and how is kept |
| Pupils who left, and their parents (if no other child is on record) | 6 years after leaving (`RECORDS_RETENTION_YEARS`), then anonymised automatically |
| Bills, payments, receipts, audit trail | Kept; anonymised as above |

## Monitoring
Error reports (Sentry, if enabled) carry no request bodies, cookies, headers, query strings or user details, and every string is redacted like the logs.
